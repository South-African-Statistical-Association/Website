Who we are

A home for statistics in South Africa

The South African Statistical Association (SASA) is the professional home for statisticians, data scientists, academics and students in South Africa. We market the discipline of statistics, build community among practitioners, support research and publication, and champion statistics education from the classroom to the workplace.

What we do

Six pillars, one mission

📊

Statistics

Marketing the discipline of statistics and improving public perception of the field.

🤝

Community

Creating forums that bring statisticians together and attract new members to the profession.

🌐

Networking

Supporting members through networking platforms, conferences and publications.

🔬

Research

Publishing research through our journal and conference proceedings.

🎓

Education

Providing financial support to students through bursaries and scholarships.

🧑‍🏫

Teaching

Hosting webinars and seminars in partnership with universities across the country.
